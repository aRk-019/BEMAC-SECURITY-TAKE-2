About the company

A security partner built for the field, not the brochure.

Bemac Security, Inc. is a security services provider committed to delivering tailored protection solutions to businesses and individuals across the country. Founded in 2025, BEMAC has built a reputation for excellence through a highly trained workforce, current technology, and a firm commitment to quality service.

The company offers a comprehensive range of services designed to safeguard people, pro ; perty, and assets — including emergency evacuation training and drills, and security risk assessment audits. Our team combines security professionals, technology specialists, and customer service staff working toward one outcome: peace of mind for our clients.
